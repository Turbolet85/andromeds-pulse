`docs/capability-record.json`, that gives each of P-001…P-082 exactly one of two dispositions: **claimed** by the
engine as it will stand, naming the 0.4.0 requirement that carries it, or **retired**, naming its surface, the
route entry that removes that surface and what guards it until then. The old gate,

the proofs of the claimed ones and carries no retired id's proof (per test-plan §9 Capability verification matrix;
architecture §Infrastructure Patterns → CI/CD approach).

its pin is one flat list of ids read by membership (inputs#I5, inputs#I6). So the form is stated here once:
**the accepted set is every entry whose `disposition` is the string `claimed`; an entry whose `disposition` is
`retired` says with what in `surfaces` and by which route entry in `removed_by`.** Nothing else in the file is
needed to derive the set.
