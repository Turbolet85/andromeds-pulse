## Goal
The CI boot smoke reads red on most runs because the app ends by itself in its first second on the runner. Two
runs kept the ending call (`ci#38019133294`, 7 of 8 boots; `ci#38022477393`, 5 of 8), and research named the cause
behind it (research.md, The cause): the app uses Xlib from two threads, GTK's main thread and a device-event thread
that `tao 0.35.0` starts on X11, and never initialises Xlib's thread support. The runner's Xlib
(`libx11-6 1.7.5-1ubuntu0.3`) does not do that when it loads; the dev host's 1.8.13 does. On the runner's Xlib a
reply is lost inside the app, Xlib raises an I/O error on a healthy connection, and GDK's handler calls `_exit(1)`.
With the runner's Xlib package swapped in on the dev host, 19 of 24 boots self-ended with the runner's call chain;
with `XInitThreads()` called first in the app process, 0 of 8.

This chunk does two things. First it repairs the series verdict's undercount: a boot that ends by itself before
the boot verb reads ready is counted `ended` and listed with its exit record and its `exit_witness` label, and the
smoke's own boot is listed the same way (per test-plan §1 Pending coverage triggers →
`harness-cleanup-verdict-and-boot-spawn-shell-coverage`; architecture §Occupied Resources → xtask CLI surfaces).
Then it closes the cause where it is, on the operator's ruling (inputs#I3, inputs#I4): the app calls
`XInitThreads()` as the second statement of `main` on Linux, right after the render-posture step, before any thread
exists and before anything reaches Xlib. The defect is in what ships: the same app ends the same way on any host whose
Xlib is older than 1.8.

**How the close is read (inputs#I1 item 2, inputs#I3).** On the runner, on equal source: three consecutive
attempts of the one pull-request run of the operator's pre-CI commit, each building the same merge commit, eight
boots an attempt, 24 boots. The without arm is the two runs before the change: 12 of 16 boots self-ended (7 of 8
and 5 of 8), a per-boot rate of 0.75 (`boot-series.json` and the witness files of both runs, research.md). At that
rate an unclosed job passes one attempt of eight boots with probability 0.25^8 = 1.5e-5 and all 24 boots with
0.25^24 = 3.6e-15. At the lower of the two runs' rates alone (5 of 8) the 24 boots pass at 6.0e-11; at the lowest
rate this job has ever read (4 of 12 first boots before the series existed, P-129's notes, not re-derived) they pass
at 5.9e-5. On the dev host the same variation is read once more by /implement on the built change: eight boots
under the runner's Xlib, which passed 0 of 4 series at the base (three at P3, and the close leg's own baseline at
P5: 7 of 8 self-ended).
