## Goal
The `ci` workflow stops running on Windows and macOS. Its two matrix jobs, `lint-test` and `a11y`, become
single-runner Linux jobs, and the `release` job, which has no Linux leg, leaves whole. The seven step conditions
that are always true on a Linux-only workflow leave with them, and so do the comments that describe the other
systems' runners. Every gate step stays, in its order (intent R7, requirement P-113; architecture §Infrastructure
Patterns → CI/CD approach; test-plan §9 Pipeline structure).

The chunk also records what one run costs, per job, before and after, read from the runs themselves (the entry;
inputs#I1 item 5). The before-reading is `ci#37934330231` on the chunk base. The removal is not expected to shorten
a round: in all eight runs read at take-up the longest job is a Linux job that stays (research.md, the per-job
table). What leaves is runner time, close to half of each run's job-seconds, and six checks.

Two things that read `ci.yml` in the tree are kept true: the workflow self-lint loses the test whose subject is the
`release` job and gains two pins, one that the workflow names no other system and one that the Linux release build
keeps its witnesses now that `release` is gone.
