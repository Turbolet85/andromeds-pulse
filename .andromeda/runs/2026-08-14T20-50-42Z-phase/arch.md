## Anti-patterns to avoid
- Do NOT instrument the dead region by exporting the product's own telemetry over OTLP to its own `:4317`/`:4318` — that is an explicit ban (infinite loop), and any "observer URL"-shaped variable must be distinct from the inbound receiver ports (per architecture.md §Established Decisions → [Self-Observation]).
- Do NOT introduce a test-only ingest path, mock channel, or feature-flagged bypass to feed the canary storm into `buffer` — synthetic telemetry enters through the real OTLP receivers only (per architecture.md §Cross-cutting Patterns → Test-time telemetry injection).
- Do NOT resolve a cross-crate wiring gap by adding a library-crate → `pulse-app` dependency, a new sibling dependency without contract justification, or a `pub` leak of `pub(crate)` internals across the `buffer`/`ingest` boundary (per architecture.md §Cross-cutting Patterns → Module dependency direction; §Conventions → Module visibility discipline).
