## Constraints
- **Workspace placement.** architecture.md §Established Decisions [Fault Identity] places the trigger line in `triage` (`triage::digest::render_payload`) and the framing instruction in `interpretation` (`interpretation::prompt::TRIGGER_FRAMING_INSTRUCTION`). The chunk's code lives in those two crates and their tests. Crate names are reserved per §Occupied Resources → Cargo workspace crate names, which is the canonical member list (§Design Philosophy → Single-process modular monolith); the keyed `§Infrastructure Patterns → Project directory structure` tree does not list either crate and is not the authority for them. No new workspace member.
- **Framing, never identity.** [Fault Identity] requires that the L2 coalesce key stay the cue tuple `(kind, scope, scope_id)` and that the trigger framing change neither the tuple, the coalesce predicate, nor the producer's title grounding. A service named on the trigger line is prompt text only.
- **Same cue as the identity.** [Fault Identity] requires the `TRIGGER:` line to be keyed on `cues.first()`, the cue the producer takes the incident identity from. The service the chunk adds must come from that same cue, not from another cue, a services row or a corpus match. Whether the code's trigger push and the producer's identity read still resolve to one cue at HEAD is research's question.
- **The recorded shape is being changed.** [Fault Identity] records the line as the cause label alone, placed directly after `OVERALL:` and absent without a cue; the instruction as ASCII, kind-generic, after `CITING_INSTRUCTION` in all three tiers' Output Instructions, conditional on a TRIGGER line, carrying a fixed set of obligations; and the prompt lineage as three per-tier version labels. Whichever of line / instruction / both the plan picks, it departs from that recorded text, so the decision entry is owed an amendment at wrap. If the instruction changes, the mandate covers all three tiers; whether the fallback and reflection tiers' labels move with the primary's is research's question.
- **Instruction stays static and generic.** [Fault Identity] requires the instruction to name no cue kind. By the same shape it should name no service literal: the service is data on the digest line, the instruction refers to it generically.
- **L4 runtime is locked.** architecture.md §Established Decisions [LLM Inference Runtime] fixes the model, the sampling operands, the committed grammar passed with `--grammar-file`, the bounded-invocation operands and their order, and requires `-p {prompt}` to stay the only OTLP-derived operand. The service name reaches the model inside the prompt only; no operand is added, removed or reordered. The schema and grammar stay byte-unchanged (§Stack and Technologies → L4 grammar conversion check binds a schema edit to a regenerated grammar; the chunk makes neither).
- **Dependency direction.** §Cross-cutting Patterns → Module dependency direction forbids a new sibling edge unless a declared contract requires it; §Conventions → Module visibility discipline keeps each crate's `pub` surface to its contract module. Whether `interpretation` already depends on `triage` (or the two halves meet only at `pulse-app`) is research's question, and decides whether a "both" form needs any shared symbol at all.

## Patterns to follow
- **Frame at composition, leave the model's text alone.** [Fault Identity] has the producer keep the model-authored symptom, timeline and ranked hypotheses as written and steers the model layer at prompt composition instead. The chunk's lever belongs on that side.
- **Conditional rendering of the trigger block.** [Fault Identity] records the line as absent without a cue, and the corpus-match note as present only when matches exist under an unchanged header. The scope's "no `scope_id` keeps the signal-only line" follows the same shape.
- **Closed label from the contract module.** The cause label comes from `triage::contract::cue_cause_label`, a closed ASCII vocabulary ([Fault Identity]; §Occupied Resources → Environment variables → `ANDROMEDA_PULSE_L4_DETERMINISTIC` lists it). The service is not a closed label; it is OTLP-derived content and is rendered beside the label, never folded into it.
- **How a real-model reading is recorded.** [Fault Identity] records each prior series with its model, runtime build, route, n per shape, grader rule and the grader's known limits, each arm run once and never re-run, reordered or re-thresholded, cited "as measured at chunk … (`evidence/…`)". The chunk's pre-registered bar and its wrap amendment follow that form. The precedent chose its lever by measurement in least-blast-radius order; this phase chooses without a model run, which the amendment should state.
- **Telemetry enters over OTLP.** §Cross-cutting Patterns → Test-time telemetry injection requires the reading's telemetry to arrive through the real receivers, with no in-process bypass.

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- Making the service on the trigger line, or anything model-authored, part of incident identity or the coalesce predicate ([Fault Identity], REJECTED: keying on `L4Output.fingerprint`).
- Grounding the first hypothesis by rewriting it at the producer after generation: [Fault Identity] grounds only the title there and leaves hypotheses as the model wrote them.
- Reading acceptance off the deterministic runner: §Occupied Resources → `ANDROMEDA_PULSE_L4_DETERMINISTIC` states its canned rank-1 hypothesis is the same for every incident, so it cannot show which service a first hypothesis names.
