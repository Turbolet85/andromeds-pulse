## Relevance
relevant — the chunk turns on one keyed contract, architecture.md §Infrastructure Patterns → CI/CD approach, and on the CI wiring recorded in §Occupied Resources → xtask CLI surfaces; it touches no workspace crate, no Standard Contract and no product resource unless research finds a reader of `ci.yml` in `xtask`.

## Constraints
- **The chunk's end state departs from the plan's own record of `ci.yml`.** Per architecture.md §Infrastructure Patterns → CI/CD approach the workflow is recorded as seven independent jobs, two of them on a three-system matrix and one (`release`) on the two non-Linux systems. The chunk leaves six jobs, all on Linux. The plan carries no record of the one-system decision (no mention of P-113 or of a Linux-only CI anywhere in it), so the authority for the change is the version intent, not this plan; the key is an expected wrap amendment and is not edited by the chunk.
- **The jobs stay independent of one another.** Per architecture.md §Infrastructure Patterns → CI/CD approach a round lasts as long as its longest job because no job waits on another; removing the `release` job and two matrix legs must add no `needs:` edge and no merged job, or the before/after per-job reading stops being comparable.
- **The Linux-only steps of `lint-test` are part of the contract, not an artefact of the matrix.** Per architecture.md §Infrastructure Patterns → CI/CD approach the Linux leg carries the perf load step, the `perf-samples` nextest profile, the `perf:budget` grader with its two required arms, and an always-run samples upload; dropping the `runner.os == 'Linux'` conditions must leave each of those steps running unconditionally (the upload keeping its always-run condition). Whether every such condition in `ci.yml` guards one of these steps and nothing else is research's question.
- **The Linux release build must keep its witness.** Per architecture.md §Infrastructure Patterns → CI/CD approach the `supply-chain` job's auditable workspace release build is named as the Linux release smoke, and `boot` builds the mcp-feature release binary before its smoke; with `release` gone these are the only release-profile builds the workflow runs, so neither may be weakened or made conditional.
- **The cache ownership recorded for Linux is unchanged; the recorded budget reading goes stale.** Per architecture.md §Infrastructure Patterns → CI/CD approach `lint-test` and `boot` own the target caches under OS-templated and Linux-named keys and save on failure, three jobs restore read-only, `coverage` caches the registry only, and `release` owns two per-OS keys. The chunk must keep the Linux keys resolving to the same strings and keep save-on-failure where the key requires it. The key's measured entry count and headroom include the two `release` entries and the non-Linux `lint-test` entries; whether those entries persist in the repository cache after their jobs leave, and what the count reads afterwards, is research's question, not a fact this plan can supply.
- **`ci.yml` is an input to a registered xtask verb.** Per architecture.md §Occupied Resources → xtask CLI surfaces `cargo xtask pre-push:linux` reads two pins out of `ci.yml`: the `node-version` major and the workflow's FIRST `apt-get install` list. The chunk's edits must leave both readable and unchanged in value, and must not reorder steps so that a different install list becomes the first. Whether `xtask/src/pre_push.rs` (or any xtask test) also pins a job name, a matrix value or a line shape that the chunk removes is research's question; if it does, the fix lives in the `xtask` crate per architecture.md §Occupied Resources → Cargo workspace crate names.
- **Three recorded wiring facts in `ci.yml` must survive the edit.** Per architecture.md §Occupied Resources → xtask CLI surfaces: every job exports `ANDROMEDA_PULSE_DATA_DIR` to the job environment from a step placed right after harden-runner (job- and workflow-level `env:` cannot carry it); the staged-artifacts gate is a plain named `run:` step directly after the capability-drift step with the workflow `permissions:` untouched; the boot smoke is the gating step of the Linux `boot` job, its three harness invocations inside one `xvfb-run`. Removing `shell: bash` from the export step is compatible with this; removing or moving the step is not.

## Patterns to follow
- **Shared CI logic that needs Rust lives in `xtask`.** Per architecture.md §Infrastructure Patterns → CI/CD approach and §Established Decisions [CI Task Runner], a check stays a `cargo xtask <task>` invocation a contributor can run locally; the chunk simplifies workflow YAML and adds no inline script in place of a removed condition.
- **Plain named `run:` steps, no new third-party action.** Per architecture.md §Occupied Resources → xtask CLI surfaces the gates are wired as plain named steps; a step that loses its `(Linux only)` suffix keeps the rest of its name, since step and job names are what the plan and other records cite.
- **OS-templated names keep their template or resolve to the same string.** Per architecture.md §Infrastructure Patterns → CI/CD approach the plan names caches and the perf-samples artifact through an `{os}` placeholder; on a single-system workflow either form is acceptable as long as the resolved name on Linux is byte-identical to today's.
- **A CI measurement is recorded with its run id and the chunk that took it.** Per architecture.md §Infrastructure Patterns → CI/CD approach, the existing cache measurements each name a `ci#…` run and a chunk marker; the per-job wall-clock record of this chunk follows that form (before and after, each tied to its run).
- **Workspace placement.** Per architecture.md §Infrastructure Patterns → Project directory structure the chunk's one file is `.github/workflows/ci.yml`; `release.yml` and `update-channels.yml` sit beside it as separate recorded workflows.

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- **Do not touch the release pipeline or its channels.** Per architecture.md §Established Decisions [Deployment / Release Pipeline] and [Distribution Channels], and §Infrastructure Patterns → CI/CD approach, `release.yml` and `update-channels.yml` are still the plan's locked distribution path; removing the `release` JOB of `ci.yml` licenses no edit to either workflow, to signing, or to bundle names.
- **Do not wire a dev-host-only verb into CI to replace what the removed legs covered.** Per architecture.md §Occupied Resources → xtask CLI surfaces, `pre-push:linux`, `perf:frame-sample` and the four `smoke:*` scenario legs are each recorded as dev-host only and not wired into CI; the frame budget in particular was taken off the hosted runners by decision.
- **Do not read the plan's three-system product statements as this chunk's work.** architecture.md §Project Intent, §Stack and Technologies and §Occupied Resources → Filesystem locations still describe a product for three operating systems (per-platform data roots, both binary suffixes, the sh/ps1 harness pair). They are product-level records, not CI wiring; this chunk changes none of what they describe and proposes no amendment to them.
