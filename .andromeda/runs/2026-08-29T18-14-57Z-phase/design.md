## Patterns to follow
- Amendment-sidecar shape: the 2026-08-21-delegated-timing-observables entry in design-system-amendments.md is the precedent for recording a signature-status change — it names the exact §sections touched, states the measured basis, and pins ownership forward to a named entry. This chunk's defer entry should follow that same section-list + basis + forward-pointer shape (target 0.4.0 residual instead of a route entry).
- Body-holds-current-truth / sidecar-holds-history split (per design-system.md closing note): the DEFERRED status belongs in the §Brand Identity and §Motion body text; the *why* and the retired ownership pointer belong in design-system-amendments.md.
- The "deferral retired as COMPLETE" pattern from 2026-08-23-a11y-verification: when a prior deferral is discharged, the body sentence naming the old owner is rewritten, not appended to. Here the inverse applies — the 2026-08-21 "build-or-retire owned by the route entry" sentence needs rewriting to point at the 0.4.0 residual.
- Measured-vs-specified vocabulary already in the body ("SPECIFIED to render… Measured 2026-08-21… the breathing/blur glow layer is unbuilt") is the established register for this signature; the defer wording should extend it rather than introduce a new status vocabulary.
- Orthogonal-axis discipline (P-004 health-vs-severity, P-026 opacity+blur-never-scale) is cited by ID in both the body and the 2026-05-29 amendment; keep citing the capability IDs when restating the deferred spec.

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- Do NOT silently delete the signature spec from §Brand Identity / §Motion / §Surface: desktop-native — the chunk requires "neither 'renders' nor silently deleted", and §Anti-Patterns Rejected Defaults explicitly rejects "uniform monochrome status icons (green checkmark / red X)" on the grounds that the Halo encodes two independent dimensions; deleting the spec would re-open that rejected default.
- Do NOT let the defer weaken the surviving dot-hue mandate into "color alone" — per §Color Palette Accent usage and §Anti-Patterns, state must never be conveyed by color alone; the deferred-halo wording must not become the only place the two-dimension encoding is described.
- Do NOT introduce new hex/px literals or a substitute visual (e.g. a generic loading spinner) while rewording the signature sites — §Self-Validation Protocol #1 (Swap Test) and #4 (Token Test) ban exactly that substitution.
