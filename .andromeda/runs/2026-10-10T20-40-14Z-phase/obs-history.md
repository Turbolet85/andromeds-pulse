- **2026-05-02** — (Initial obs plan, Phase 3 synthesis) fixed the split the extract's dependency constraint rests on: self-observation is `tracing` only, and the external OTLP side is `tonic` / `axum` / `prost` / `opentelemetry-proto`, parse-only with no SDK runtime. - **2026-05-04** — (Clarify PII grep heuristic UI-vocabulary exemption) a log grep for secrets must match secret formats, not the words "token" / "password" / "API key". A test that reads `rows_ingested` / `append_rejections` / `redactions_applied` from `buffer.tick`, as the extract's pattern and its third acceptance line ask, waits for a tick; the `health` command is a different signal and carries none of those fields.
