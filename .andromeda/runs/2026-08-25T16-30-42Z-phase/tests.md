## Anti-patterns to avoid
- NEVER let a human-approved step be the pass/fail signal (no manual smoke step, no human-reviewed output as the gate) — the operator's brief judgment is additive evidence beside a machine half (per test-plan §11 Universal; §2).
- NEVER use `sleep(N)` for synchronization in the sustained scenario — wait on an explicit signal (health polling, obs record, channel event); flakes are quarantine-and-root-cause, never retry (per test-plan §11 E2E; §10 Zero-flakiness budget).
- NEVER seed from production telemetry dumps, `.sql` scripts, or pre-baked DB snapshots when widening the injector's data generation (per test-plan §11 Test Data).
