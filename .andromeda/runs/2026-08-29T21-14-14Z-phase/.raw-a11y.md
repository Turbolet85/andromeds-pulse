## Anti-patterns to avoid
- NEVER soft-gate, waive, or defer a11y to post-merge (a11y-plan §11 CI: "a11y is FAIL-fast gate, not warning"; "no fix in follow-up") — a dependency-churn chunk under lockfile pressure is exactly where this gets rationalized.
- NEVER apply a retry-once policy to an a11y failure that appears after the bumps (a11y-plan §11 SLO + §10) — quarantine, investigate, fix or delete; retry masks a real regression.
- NEVER bump or swap the a11y tool stack opportunistically alongside the Cargo work, and NEVER end up with both ARIA component libraries present (a11y-plan §11 Universal: never mix react-aria-components and headless-ui; §3 pins the tool versions).
