- **Modal overlay pattern:** overlay + inner card + close button (top-right `✕` glyph, `color-text-secondary` → `color-text-primary` hover over `duration-fast`) — composing the chunk #37 Modal primitive scaffold as the structural shell (per layout-templates.md §Component — Investigation modal and §Component — Settings modal (form)).
- **Form field grouping pattern:** each logical group (theme, widget-position, retention, MCP, snapshot-preset, plugin-manager) as a discrete stacked section separated by `gap: space-lg` — matches the vertical stacking principle established for the Settings panel entry in §Primary screens.
- **Widget snap position control pattern:** 3×3 grid of radio buttons representing corners + edges + center with a visual preview of widget position on screen (per layout-templates.md §Component — Settings modal (form) Form controls — widget snap position).
- **Tab-order pattern from titlebar through to window controls:** the settings gear icon (aperture glyph) in the custom titlebar initiates the modal; focus moves into the modal at first focusable field (theme selector), traverses the form, and terminates at Save/Cancel (per layout-templates.md §Component — Custom titlebar and §Keyboard navigation).

- Do NOT apply custom animation timings other than `duration-standard` for modal panel open/close — the `0.35` webview expression budget and stated `duration-standard` ease constrain this surface (per layout-templates.md §Decisions Log Motion trigger placement).
