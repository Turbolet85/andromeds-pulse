This is the definition behind the §10 heartbeat-gap CI gate (≤ 45 000 ms per `{ingest,buffer,viz,plugins}.tick`, `xtask/ci/heartbeat-gap-check.sh`). A relocated gate, or a WSL pre-push boot leg, must read the tick gap from the JSONL log of its own run, not substitute the `health` poll.
