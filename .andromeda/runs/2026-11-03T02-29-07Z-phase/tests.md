## Patterns to follow
- Spawned-program witness form (test-plan §3 `Per-chunk gate discipline`): cleared environment, own TempDir data dir, ports claimed through `test_support::ports::claim_port` (never port 0 bound and released, and never 4317/4318), pid from `Child::id()`, records awaited by target under a bound, and the log family read after the end.
- Door test depths and readings (test-plan §1 Surfaces under test, door row): a `tokio::net::UnixStream` client asserts the answer line or a fixed error code, plus the engine's own `door.call` / `door.refused` records. Model the rewrites on `pulse-app/tests/unit_door.rs`, `integration_engine_boot.rs` (door arms) and `integration_door.rs`.
- Telemetry seeding from the `test_support::otlp` factories, with no hand-built literals and no pre-baked data (test-plan §7 Self-bootstrapping requirement; test-plan §4 Fixture pattern at unit level).
- In-process tool dispatch over the tools library (`call_tool`, with `dispatch_tool` calling it) as the unit-tier carrier that stays (test-plan §1 Coverage scope, mcp-server row).

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- NEVER use `sleep(N)` for synchronization in the rewritten spawned tests. Wait for an explicit record or port signal (test-plan §11 E2E).
- NEVER lower the coverage threshold to pass. Deleting the sidecar and its tests moves the measure, and the gate stays ≥ 75% line / ≥ 85% function (test-plan §11 CI; test-plan §10 Coverage thresholds).
- NEVER mock DuckDB or over-mock at integration level. The rewritten cross-process tests should read the engine's real stores through the door (test-plan §11 Integration).
