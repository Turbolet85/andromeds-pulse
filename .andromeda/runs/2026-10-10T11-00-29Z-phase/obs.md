## Patterns to follow
- A gate's verdict has three exits — pass, fail, cannot-evaluate on an absent input — per obs-plan §10 CI gates (the `ci-gates` and `perf:budget` bullets). The re-pointed capability gate reading an absent or empty record follows the same shape; the obs plan states it for log-reading gates only (see Contract bindings).
- A gate prints what it read (a count of records over a count of files) beside its verdict, per obs-plan §10 CI gates (the perf-budget bullet's measured line), so a job log shows the gate read something. The gate's present `{n}/60 capabilities` line is the capability gate's form of it; its count must follow the record, not a constant.
- Where the record or the plan states what guards an id, name the grader verb and the signal as the obs plan spells them (obs-plan §8 PII Scrubbing, the three DELEGATED TIMING leaves; obs-plan §5 Metric Coverage, the PII-redaction row), not a paraphrase, so the external reader and the wrap can match them.
- A claim about a reading carries its run or chunk and its limit, the form obs-plan §9 CI Integration (Telemetry artifact handling) and obs-plan §10 CI gates use throughout ("as measured on …", "one run measured"). A "still guarded by its tests" statement in the record takes the same form or is left to research.

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- A tolerance for an absent input is never a licence for a gate to pass over it — obs-plan §10 DuckDB Connection Isolation & Load-Profile Operational Constraints (Load-profile constraints). A capability gate that reads no record, or a record with no claimed id, must not print a pass.
- NEVER rely on colored terminal output for CI parsing — obs-plan §11 Obs Anti-Patterns → CI. The gate's verdict stays a plain line an agent reads from the job log.
- NEVER skip spans on critical user-facing paths (P1–P7) — obs-plan §11 Obs Anti-Patterns → Telemetry Strategy. While the window, the model and the desktop surfaces stand in the tree, their instrumentation stands with them; a retirement in the record removes none of it.
