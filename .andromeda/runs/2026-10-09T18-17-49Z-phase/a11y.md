## Constraints
- a11y-plan §9 CI Integration (Pipeline integration) requires the a11y CI to reuse tests' `boot` / `run` / `status` / `cleanup` / `logs` harness. A change to what `boot` means by ready is a change to a verb a11y is declared to share: the verb's exit contract and output shape stay usable by an a11y consumer. Whether any a11y spec or the `a11y` job actually invokes `boot` is research's question.
- a11y-plan §3 Focus management test harness (Contract binding) requires the focus harness to reuse the same 5-command driver. A readiness wait added to `boot` is inherited by that harness wherever it is wired to it; whether it is wired to it in code is research's question.
- a11y-plan §3 CI integration (Pipeline integration) requires the `a11y` job to be its own blocking job in `ci.yml` on `ubuntu-22.04`, parallel with the test jobs, with its two artifacts uploaded `if: always()`. An edit to the boot job in the same file leaves that job's steps, its blocking status and its uploads as they are.
- a11y-plan §10 SLO Invariants & A11y Budgets (always-required invariant) requires no retry-once policy, stated as parallel to tests' zero-flakiness budget. A closure that makes the smoke green by re-attempting a boot is outside that parallel; a wait for a stated readiness condition is not a retry.
- a11y-plan §10 SLO Invariants & A11y Budgets (Performance budget per a11y CI run) requires the a11y CI run plus tests E2E to stay under its stated total. A longer `boot` wait or a longer-lived smoke counts against it only if the a11y job runs `boot`; research's question, as above.
- a11y-plan §1 A11y Scope Summary (A11y surfaces & assistive tech reach) names Orca and the Linux ATK APIs as the assistive path for the webview and the tray on Linux. The plan holds no requirement about the accessibility bus under a virtual display, so it neither explains nor excuses the boot log's accessibility-bus warning; whether that warning bears on the early exit is research's question. If the closure turns the accessibility bridge off, that setting stays in the harness or CI environment and never becomes a default of the product binary.
- a11y-plan §10 SLO Invariants & A11y Budgets (Standard+ invariants, zero new violations per PR) applies if the cause is closed inside the window: a closure that changes the rendered DOM of any P1–P14 surface (a11y-plan §1, Surface set extension) is graded by the per-PR regression gate like any other webview change. A closure outside `pulse-app/ui/` does not engage it.

## Patterns to follow
- The a11y suite audits a statically served SPA over a mocked Tauri IPC layer (a11y-plan §3 Harness wiring & conventions, Mock layer and Static serving). By the plan, a11y assertions do not need a live app to reach ready; treat that as the reason a `boot` change is expected to be a11y-neutral, and have research confirm it against the job's steps.
- The a11y job keeps its evidence on a failed run through `if: always()` uploads (a11y-plan §3 CI integration, Pipeline integration). It is the in-file precedent for the chunk's "the job keeps what would show the cause".
- A flaking check is quarantined, investigated, then fixed or deleted before it is unquarantined; it is never retried into green (a11y-plan §11 A11y Anti-Patterns, SLO).
- If the diff touches `pulse-app/ui/`, suite health is checked with the config-named Playwright list command, never the bare one (a11y-plan §3 Harness wiring & conventions, Adding a surface).

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- NEVER a retry-once policy that masks a real failure (a11y-plan §11 A11y Anti-Patterns, SLO).
- NEVER an a11y gate softened, skipped or made conditional by a CI edit made for another job (a11y-plan §11 A11y Anti-Patterns, CI).
- NEVER important content hidden from assistive tech (a11y-plan §11 A11y Anti-Patterns, Screen Reader) — the product-level reading of an accessibility bridge switched off by default.
