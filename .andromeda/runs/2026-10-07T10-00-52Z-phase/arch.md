## Patterns to follow
- Lever chosen by measurement, in least-blast-radius order: the plan records the framing sentence as selected over a conventions sentence and a schema-description sentence, each arm run once, the first qualifier shipped and no combination run. The remedy candidates here (cap or reorder the corpus block, restate the obligation after it, exclude other scopes' history) follow the same order-and-stop shape (per architecture.md §Established Decisions [Fault Identity]).
- One pre-registered reading, fired once, never re-run, re-ordered or re-thresholded, with the grader and the bar named before the run. The plan records the grader as the probe's `identifies` label over the first hypothesis statement only (the `scope_id` as a whole word, neither neighbour in `[a-z0-9_-]`, AND a retry token) and the bar as `both` ≥ 36/40 over S1–S4 and ≥ 19/20 over S7 + S8, with a regression guard against the baseline arm. Whether the probe's grader at HEAD is that grader is research's question (per architecture.md §Established Decisions [Fault Identity]).
- Harness-only renders: a candidate render can be measured in the probe as an arm without entering the product (the plan records the line-bearing `L` / `LI` arms and a probe-only enriched render as such). A candidate that fails or is out of bounds stays a probe arm (per architecture.md §Established Decisions [Fault Identity]).
- State what a reading does not show beside what it shows. The plan records that the baseline arm met the bar on every existing shape, so the probe held no known-positive for the end-to-end miss; this chunk's first half is that gap, and its result is written in the same two-sided form (per architecture.md §Established Decisions [Fault Identity]).
- The `LlmInferenceRunner` trait is the swap boundary and the concrete `llama-cli` impl lives at the binary boundary; the readings need no trait change. Whether the probe generates through the trait or spawns on its own is research's question (per architecture.md §Established Decisions [LLM Inference Runtime]).

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- Putting the `scope_id` or a service name on the `TRIGGER:` line, or naming a service by value in the instruction text, to make the reproducing shape pass (per architecture.md §Established Decisions [Fault Identity]).
- Simplifying the `fingerprint_match` retrieval arm away, keying anything on `L4Output.fingerprint`, or encoding a fingerprint with the 4-byte tracing prefix while reshaping the corpus block (per architecture.md §Established Decisions [Fault Identity]).
- Standing the deterministic runner in for a real-model reading: under `ANDROMEDA_PULSE_L4_DETERMINISTIC` the canned rank-1 hypothesis names a retry storm for every incident, so it can neither reproduce the miss nor grade a remedy (per architecture.md §Occupied Resources → Environment variables).
