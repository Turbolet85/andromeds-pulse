## Relevant amendment history
- **2026-05-04 — Clarify PII grep heuristic UI-vocabulary exemption** — a PII grep must tell secret *formats* (regex envelopes) apart from UI vocabulary words such as "token". This bears on the committed-`evidence/` hygiene check. The probe's stdout and `runs.json` can carry words like "tokens", "t/s" or "prompt" as labels or throughput units. A no-model-text / no-secret grep over evidence must match content formats, not those words, or it will report false positives.
- **2026-06-10 — Chunk #99 tag gate: frame-budget posture + L4 budgets + load-suite findings** — confirmed the per-hardware-profile L4 latency budgets in `xtask/ci/l4-latency-p99` as canonical, with no re-derivation. Any throughput or latency the real-model CUDA run observes (the gate cites 155 t/s from the overseer's smoke) is read against those budgets as recorded context. It is not a new budget, and this chunk does not re-derive one.
- **2026-08-15-corpus-key-persistence** (secret env-var naming convention) — added `*_PASSPHRASE` to the redacted-name suffixes (`*_SECRET` / `*_TOKEN` / `*_KEY`).
