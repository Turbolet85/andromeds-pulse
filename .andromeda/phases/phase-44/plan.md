- **`plugins.invoke` is capability-handshake-only at chunk #47**: per research §Open question #1, chunk #47 does NOT run the wasm component's actual export function. The `invoke` method validates that the requested `capability` name matches one of the plugin's category WIT-declared exports (custom-dashboard: `render`; data-transform: `transform`; snapshot-template: `render-template`) and returns the handshake result. Reset only at process restart. A future chunk introducing real export invocation may add a concurrent gauge for "currently-executing" semantics if profiling exposes need; chunk #47 ships the simpler cumulative semantics.

The loader iterates the 3 known categories explicitly; files dropped at the top-level `plugins/` dir (not in a category subdir) are silently ignored (log a `warn` event at `plugin.load.discover.unknown_category` with the basename, no full path).
