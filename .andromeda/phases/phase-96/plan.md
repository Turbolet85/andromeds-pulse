## Implementation notes

- **Order sensitivity:** step 1 (reconciliation) gates everything; step 2-3 (matrix) and step 4 (load suite) are independent; step 6 (a11y extension) before re-baselining; step 8 (plan amendments) after the verification reality is known; step 9 last.
- **No new env vars / no bare `#[ignore]`** for load-profile gating — use the nextest profile/filter mechanism; verify `quarantine-tracking-check` semantics at step 1 and pick the shape that keeps it green. If nextest's per-profile `default-filter` is unavailable in the pinned version, fall back to a filter-expression invocation inside `cargo xtask perf:load-profiles` (tests excluded from `ci` profile via nextest.toml overrides).
- **Frame budget binding:** all assertions bind to obs-plan §10 ms-values (p99 ≤33ms); the test-plan §10 fps row (≥20 fps p99 = 50ms) is numerically weaker and gets reconciled in the test-plan Decisions Log entry, not asserted (combined rot warning 5).
- **Remediation rule:** violations surfaced by the re-audit are fixed in-chunk (that is the point of a gate chunk) UNLESS the fix needs a registry delta or a structural arch change — then halt and surface (amendment path), preserving the zero-delta declaration.
- **NEUTRAL→ACTIVE semantics:** CI keeps NEUTRAL passes for frame/L4 gates (no webview/model there); the ACTIVE evidence for the tag gate is produced locally via the step-5 booted window. Document both states in the tag-gate report so "pass" is never ambiguous.
- **Manual SR passes** are supplemental user-produced evidence (NVDA/VoiceOver/Orca per `screen-reader/` specs); the plan records their status honestly — pending manual passes do not block the automated gate set but are listed in the tag-gate report.
- **Five open questions for Phase 6 review** (from research.md): (1) tauri-driver headful — recommend keep-deferred + document; (2) load-profile placement — recommend xtask local/release-gate, CI keeps smoke; (3) tag push is OUT of chunk scope (signing blockers); (4) L4 per-profile coverage limits on this host; (5) frame ACTIVE evidence is local-only. Defaults are encoded in the steps above; user can override at review.
