## Goal

Make the retry-storm detector's feed actually reachable by a storm of identical exception span-events delivered over OTLP, and prove it with the detector's own latched counters. An outside party measured thirty-one storm-tick lines across three launch arms with every fingerprint field zero, while Pulse's ingest counter showed all nine spans arriving and holding — spans arrive, nothing reaches the fingerprint observer. The region between OTLP receipt and the per-span-event fingerprint hook is where that stops, and no artifact records what happens inside it, because the corpus holds no span table.

Research narrowed that region substantially without settling it. The wiring premise the hand-off could only assert from source is confirmed at HEAD — `main.rs:600-604` constructs `Some(StormObserverAdapter)` and `main.rs:1110` passes it into `run_consumer`, which threads it unconditionally to the per-event fan-out at `appender.rs:394-400`. The security extract's hypothesis that the mandated PII scrub might starve the fingerprint is falsified: `appender.rs:358` computes it from the raw type and stacktrace *before* the scrub, and `exception_type` is never scrubbed. And the buffer-crate path is exercised end-to-end by an existing e2e (`e2e_storm_detection.rs`) that drives a real export request through to the Suggested/Autonomous thresholds. What research newly surfaced is a boot-time branch — `match buffer_conn` at `main.rs:1102`, whose `None` arm spawns a receiver-drain that discards every batch while leaving ingest's counter incrementing — a mechanism that reproduces the measured signature exactly. That is a candidate, not a verdict.

So the chunk runs in that order: close the deferred Rust gate to learn whether the guarding e2e is even green at HEAD, make the feed positively observable rather than inferable from silence, capture it live under a canary storm, localize, repair, and guard. The instrumentation ships permanently (operator-directed at P4): three atomic counters on `BufferState` surfaced on the existing 15s `buffer.tick`, plus a one-time degraded-boot warn — so the next regression of this class announces itself instead of presenting as absence. Self-observation stays `tracing`-only and aggregate-only per obs-plan §3/§5/§8; nothing added here carries an OTLP attribute value.

## Constraints & rejected approaches
- **Do not treat `tracked_fingerprints_count` as the acceptance signal** — it is a windowed, post-eviction, distinct-count gauge; a working storm reads `1`, and `0` after the window. Reading it as a latched total is the misreading that made the original evidence ambiguous.
- **Do not remove or reorder the fingerprint-before-scrub sequence** at `appender.rs:352-367` to simplify the counters — the ordering is deliberate and commented; scrubbing first would collide unrelated exceptions on the redaction marker.
- **Do not instrument by emitting per span-event** — the appender loop is a named hot path; counters are atomics, surfaced on the existing 15s tick. Rejected: a per-event `debug` line (unbounded volume at 10k spans/s, and the very field-formatting cost obs-plan §11 bans).
- **Do not add a new `pulse://stream/*` topic or tick target for detector visibility** — the existing `buffer.tick` already carries the module's counters. Rejected because a new topic is an arch §Occupied Resources registration for no gain here.
- **Do not run this chunk's capture against Conductor** — the scope needs no incident and no MCP read-back, and the hand-off's §5 confirms the leg is tractable without them. `inject_demo` is the local canary. The Conductor leg belongs to P-075.
- **Do not weaken ingest's invariant checks to unblock the feed** even if localization implicates them — that inverts a security invariant to fix an observability defect.
- **Do not put the guard test in `pulse-app/src/*.rs` as `#[cfg(test)] mod tests`** — `[lib] test = false` means it compiles under clippy but never runs; it would report green while proving nothing.
