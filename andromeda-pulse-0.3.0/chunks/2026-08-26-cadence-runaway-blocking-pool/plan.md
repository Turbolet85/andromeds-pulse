## Goal

The chunk's title states a causal direction its own evidence contradicts, and P3 measured the correction:
the cue storm begins at 17:21:22, **32 seconds after** the consumer's last append at 17:20:50.787, and the
entire 66-second pre-freeze window carried 11 cadence cycles and 110 L1a queries — about 1.7 blocking
queries per second against a 512-thread pool. **The runaway is an amplifier that makes recovery impossible,
not the initiating cause.** Per the operator's P4 decision the marker and the working-route entry stay as
the historical record; `scope.md` carries the corrected mechanism (amended at P3 closure) and the master
desc will record the measured actuals at the flip.

So this chunk breaks the amplifier and instruments the initiator. The vicious cycle is fully traced:
`observe_spans_for_baseline` runs INSIDE `run_consumer` (`crates/buffer/src/consumer.rs:47-56`), so a wedged
consumer starves `BaselineState` too; ~32 s later every tracked service crosses its silence threshold and the
emitter produces one `service_went_silent` cue per service per tick — 5 × 60 = the observed flat 300/min,
with 4,590 of the run's 4,599 cues being that single kind. `emit_cue` then fans every **Suggested** cue 1:1
into a cadence trigger with no rate limit (`crates/triage/src/cue/emitter.rs:189-192`), and each trigger
drives Q1–Q7 as ten `spawn_blocking` calls (ratio measured at 9.996 across the run and 10.0 pre-freeze).

**Stated plainly and not smoothed over: this chunk does NOT prevent the initiating freeze.** It makes the
freeze survivable and the next occurrence diagnosable. The initiator gets its own route entry at wrap.
