## Goal

Under a moderate sustained OTLP feed the buffer consumer stops draining permanently and nothing says so:
`buffer.tick.rows_ingested` froze at 2187 across 56 ticks while `ingest.tick.buffer_capacity_pct` climbed to
100 % and pinned, 27,675 spans were accepted and never persisted, and the run's own log carried **0 ERROR and
0 `app.panic.fatal`** with heartbeats ticking throughout. This chunk makes the buffer keep draining and makes a
wedged consumer visible instead of silent.

Research settled why nothing said so, and it is structural rather than an oversight. `health` reports
LIVENESS, not PROGRESS — `crates/ui-bridge/src/health.rs:190-192` drives every subsystem's status from
`HeartbeatState` **tick** timestamps, and the ticks never stopped. The `ready` envelope already carries both
numbers a detector needs (`ingest_mpsc_capacity_pct`, `rows_ingested`) but nothing correlates them, and
`BufferState` records no last-append instant at all. So obs-plan §3/§10's stall definition (tick absence
>45s, gated by `heartbeat-gap-check.sh`) is liveness-positive and progress-negative here: the condition is
outside what any shipped mandate detects.

Research also found a named, documented-invariant violation on the same seam. obs-plan §10 requires that *"any
future DuckDB consumer with a multi-second statement MUST take a dedicated `try_clone()` connection, never the
shared appender connection"* — and `crates/viz/src/query.rs` locks the **shared** connection at `:114` / `:211`
/ `:307`, with **no `try_clone` anywhere in the crate** and no timeout at its `spawn_blocking` call sites
(`pulse-app/src/viz_routers.rs:32,63,94`). Since `2026-07-07-traces-table-auto-refresh` those polls are
periodic, not user-driven. A second violator: the Q7 fallback runs on the shared connection, untimed, exactly
when the database is already slow (`crates/triage/src/baseline/sql.rs:490`). Retention, Q1–Q6 and the Q7
primary honour the invariant. Per the operator's P4 decision, this chunk closes both violations on their own
merits — but **attribution is still owed on evidence**, not assumed: the RED leg names the holder before any
fix lands.

Per operator decision the observable is enforced, not merely emitted: a field pair, a transition WARN on its
own exact allowlist leaf, and an xtask check that FAILS. This project has twice recorded that a check the
harness runs beats a discipline an author must remember.

## Constraints & rejected approaches
- **No new backpressure policy** (dropping, sampling, spill-to-disk) — `try_send` → `ResourceExhausted` is
  arch-declared behaviour and stays. Spill-to-disk additionally would create an unencrypted at-rest store of
  client-controlled telemetry needing a security-plan §Data Protection row before it could ship.
- **No env knob for the stall threshold** — obs-plan §10 already fixes the tolerance numbers; a knob would buy
  nothing and cost two registry registrations.
- **No in-process test bypass** to prove draining — telemetry enters over real OTLP on `:4317`/`:4318` only
  (arch §Cross-cutting Patterns → Test-time telemetry injection). No mock of DuckDB or of the boundary under
  test.
- **No `sleep(N)` for synchronization** — wait on an explicit signal (log-line match, `health`/`ready` poll).
  Acute here: the condition is time-shaped and a fixed sleep converts a wedge assertion into a flake, against
  an absolute zero-flakiness budget.
- **Do not absorb the muted-diagnostics sweep.** If ruling the L4 candidate in or out needs
  `interpretation.model.load → inference_mode`, un-mute exactly that one target and say so; the rest belongs
  to the `Diagnostics un-muting + harness-truth sweep` entry.
- **Do not touch the L4 argv-prompt or the three unconfined path vars** — owned by the `L4 runtime security
  residuals` entry. They are candidate evidence here, not this chunk's fix.
- **Rejected: raising `max_blocking_threads`.** The runtime leaves it at tokio's default 512
  (`pulse-app/src/main.rs:276-279`). Raising it enlarges the queue in front of a blocked holder without
  unblocking it — it would postpone the wedge and make it harder to observe, which is the opposite of this
  chunk's second deliverable.

## Implementation notes
- **Attribution order is load-bearing.** Steps 3–4 close a documented invariant violation and are justified
  independently; they are NOT a claim about the cause. Step 1 must name the holder before step 5, and the
  report must be explicit if the measured cause lies outside steps 3–4.
- `BufferState` is lock-free (`AtomicU64` throughout) and must stay so — a mutex there would put a new lock on

  (`crates/triage/src/baseline/sql.rs:441-447`). Any timeout added must pair with an `interrupt_handle()` on a
  cloned connection, as `run_q7_with_timeout` already does.
