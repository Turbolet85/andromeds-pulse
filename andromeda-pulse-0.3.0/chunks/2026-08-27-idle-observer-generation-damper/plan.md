## Goal
A quiet system stops burning standing GPU. The measured 11h08m idle record shows every digest — 3,011 tier1 (cue-driven at the 60s latch rate) plus 666 no-cue tier3 baselines — spawning a full L4 generation although nothing changed, 39.1% GPU duty on zero ingest, and auto-resolve defeated by churn (59 incidents/day, ~5 active permanent) because every deduped re-generation bumps `updated_at` (per scope §Problem; mechanisms verified at research).

The fix, per the operator's composition ruling (this phase's P4 dialogue): a single **unchanged-input gate at the L4 subscriber**.
