## Goal
On a freshly booted app, a service's first span must put its constellation dot on screen within P-027's ≤ 5 s
bound. Today the dot waits for the service registry's first 15 s lifecycle tick (measured first rise 9 986 ms at
2026-09-29-p-025-hue-shift-observable-made-gradable). The cause is in the discovery path. `ServiceRegistry` gains
entries only inside `tick_all` (heartbeat), a restart, an override or a corpus restore, never on span arrival
(`crates/triage/src/lifecycle/registry.rs:205-345`), and the heartbeat skips its immediate first tick
(`lifecycle/mod.rs:96`). The fix registers a service at its FIRST SIGHTING, through a third `SpanObserver`
adapter at the pulse-app boundary. The adapter consumes the scrubbed name the buffer tap already passes, respects
the baseline's `ACTIVITY_FLOOR_SERVICE_CAP`, and performs the same `Unknown → Bootstrapping` / `Activity`
transition the tick would have performed, only earlier. The 5 s budget, the heartbeat cadence and the P-027
observable are not changed (per arch §Cross-cutting Patterns → Module dependency direction; security-plan
§Security Anti-Patterns → Logging, the `extract_service_name` choke point; obs-plan §8 delegated-timing leaves).

A new dev-host scenario leg, `cargo xtask smoke:discovery`, proves the bound against the real release binary on a
fresh data dir. It grades the interval from the first `duckdb.append {table_name: "spans"}` record (the
first-sighting instant) to the first `metric.constellation.discovery_ms` record (the paint), and it requires the
discovery record's own anchor to land on that first-sighting instant. It reads RED at the chunk base and GREEN
after the fix (per test-plan §3 Direct-binary smoke variant + the `smoke:hue-shift` paragraph). The P-025 hue
observable keeps its anchor and emission rule, and `cargo xtask smoke:hue-shift` must still PASS. Its fresh-boot
rise value is predicted to shorten, because the dot now exists before the incident opens.

Research falsified one scope premise, recorded in scope.md. At HEAD the P-027 mark's anchor
(`last_seen_unix_nano`) is stamped at tick time (`registry.rs:333-341`), so the discovery record cannot see the
gap it bounds. After the fix a brand-new service's first appearance has `last_seen` = its first-sighting
instant, so the existing mark then measures first-seen-to-dot for the case this chunk grades.
