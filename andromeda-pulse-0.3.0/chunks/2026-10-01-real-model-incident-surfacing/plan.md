## Constraints & rejected approaches
- **Retuning until a sample passes** — rejected by the founder's directive. Step 12's rule and its one combination run
  are the whole search.
- **The overseer's approval conditions (P5, 2026-10-01) are binding:**
  - The pre-registered decision rule is the contract, and A0 is recorded on untouched code first.
  - Arms are never reordered or re-run to chase 27/30.
  - Every arm's count is reported.
  - A1 or A3 selected → stop for the founder's live word.
  - Slot 1 has no window and no port, but it is heavy CPU while a founder-started `cargo mutants` audit runs, so ask
    before it anyway.
  - Slot 2 is the operator's :4317 grant.
- **Changing the creation predicate** (e.g. creating incidents on `watch` or on `severity: none`) — rejected. It decides
  whether the model's decision is honoured, not why the model decides, and `dismiss`/`none` → no incident is the
  decided semantics.
- **Keying anything on `L4Output.fingerprint`** — rejected (arch §Fault Identity).
- **Swapping the model or llama.cpp build, or dropping the GBNF constraint** — rejected (arch-locked).
- **A measurement-only `#[test]`** — rejected: a committed test must give a deterministic verdict.
- **An xtask probe leg** — rejected: xtask cannot link the runner.
- **Committing `runs.json` or any raw tally to the tree** — rejected (validation check 4 (7)). Per-arm summary lines
  only, in `evidence/`.
- **Logging model text (title, symptom, justification) to make a dismissal "debuggable"** — rejected (obs-plan §11;
  security-plan §Logging). Bounded labels only.
- **Captured telemetry as probe input** — rejected (test-plan §11). The three digests are synthetic.
- **Running the probe or `pulse-app` without the operator's slot** — never. Each slot is asked for with its stated
  length, and ports 4317/4318 are shared with conductor-builder.
