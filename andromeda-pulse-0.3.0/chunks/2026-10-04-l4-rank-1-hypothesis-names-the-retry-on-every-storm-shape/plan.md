## Constraints & rejected approaches
- Putting the service name, error rate or p99 into the TRIGGER line — rejected. The overseer's kind-label-only ruling
  stands (arch history, `2026-10-04-l4-interpretation-names-its-triggering-cue`), and non-closed content would widen
  the argv boundary (security history).
- Changing the verdict grader (adding `retries` to `trigger_terms`) — rejected at P4. It would move the instrument the
  bar was defined on after a seen FAIL. The stem reading is record-only.
- Rewriting the rank-1 hypothesis at the producer (post-hoc) — rejected. Model-authored hypotheses are never rewritten
  at the producer (arch [Fault Identity]); the remedy steers at prompt composition only.
- Candidate texts naming `retry` — rejected. The framing is kind-generic ("the TRIGGER line's own words"), so the
  remedy cannot be a retry-specific word plant that the grader rewards.
- Re-running any slot, reordering arms, or tuning text between Slot 1 and Step 7 — forbidden. The shipped text is the
  measured constant byte for byte. Any further run is a new pre-registration.
- Digest re-ordering (moving ATTENTION CUES above SERVICES) — not a candidate. It has a larger blast radius
  (`render_payload` and its pins, the PII scrub fixture) than the three prompt-text levers, and no measured basis.
- Forcing the tier with `ANDROMEDA_PULSE_HARDWARE_PROFILE` — rejected. Detection routes the slots, as at the
  predecessor, so the result is comparable.
