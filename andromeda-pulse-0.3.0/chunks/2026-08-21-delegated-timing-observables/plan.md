## Goal

Three delegated timing bounds — P-025 halo hue ≤ 2s, P-027 constellation discovery ≤ 5s, P-045 findings-counter
refresh ≤ 1s — have no measurement surface on the Pulse side. All three carry the same `notes` clause in
`docs/v0_2_0/capability-verification-matrix.json`: the bound is delegated to Conductor dynamic verification and
is *"intentionally NOT faked as a sleep-based unit test"*, while each cap's static half is already automated.
Conductor's Epoch 4 blocks on exactly these three; P-037 is the fourth delegated cap and is already observable
(`metric.report.render_ms`), which is why it is the shape precedent rather than a target.

This chunk builds the missing observables and nothing else. Each of the three end instants is a paint inside
the webview — a severity-driven LCH hue (per design-system §Brand Identity "Signature element"), a constellation
dot's first paint, a counter's committed refresh — so the measurement is necessarily webview-originated, and
obs-plan §1/§4 makes that a rule rather than a preference: a webview-originated measurement reaches the log
**only** through a `telemetry.frontend.*` TauRPC command that emits backend-side. The existing
`record_frame_ms` → `metric.webgpu.frame_duration_ms` path is that rule's one worked example, and research
confirmed it already emits from the reduced-motion static-paint branch — which is what design's
`prefers-reduced-motion` constraint and a11y-plan §6/§11 demand of any new mark.

The chunk does **not** assert the bounds. Asserting them is Conductor-side and belongs to P-075, which stays
pooled by operator decision (2026-08-21): its acceptance is `dynamic-external` and additionally needs a
Conductor assertion round, so claiming it here would produce a hollow `verified`.

## Constraints & rejected approaches
- **Backend-only timestamps — REJECTED.** All three end instants are paints inside the webview and are invisible to the Rust side; a backend measure would time a different thing and make the bound unfalsifiable. obs-plan §1/§4 independently forbids it.
- **One parameterized procedure — REJECTED at P4 (operator).** The three surfaces carry different fields, so a single flat input needs optionals and "required when surface=X" cannot be expressed in the type.
- **A payload-carrying enum argument — REJECTED as unverified.** Cleanest on paper, but research did not check how taurpc 0.7 + specta emit enum-with-payload across the TS bindings; adopting it would mean discovering that during implementation.
- **Do NOT widen the bare `"metric"` allowlist key** to cover the three targets — it would silently widen every sibling `metric.*` target to one field set.
- **Do NOT label any event by service name.** P-027 is per-service on the dashboard surface, but `service.name` is an OTLP resource attribute (user-controlled content); the measure must be aggregate — a count plus a duration.
- **Do NOT assert the timing bounds here** — no `sleep`, no retry, no real-clock read. That is Conductor's half.
- **Do NOT take a mark at animation/transition end** — it would make the measurement motion-dependent and read differently under reduced-motion emulation.
- **Do NOT add ARIA or a hidden DOM node as a measurement hook** — marks stay non-semantic (a11y-plan §11).
