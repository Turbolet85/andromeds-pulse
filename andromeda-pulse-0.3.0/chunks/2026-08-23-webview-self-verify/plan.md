## Goal

Stand up a driver that lets an agent **press a real control in the live Tauri window on this Windows host**,
and prove it with one canonical leg. Today nothing in either repo can do that: `cargo xtask self-verify` is
boot-quit by construction and "NEVER CLICKS a user-facing control" (`.claude/rules/verification-harness.md:110`),
while the Playwright a11y suite drives a static `dist` in Chromium via `http-server` (`playwright-a11y.config.ts`)
— never the app. The gap is the driver, not the intent.

The canonical leg is the **custom-titlebar ✕ on the compact widget**. That control is the exact one
`verification-harness.md:110` records as matrix-`verified` under P-063 while doing NOTHING — `getCurrentWindow().close()`
silently dropped by the negative-default ACL. **It works today** (`pulse-app/capabilities/default.json` grants
`core:window:allow-close`), so the leg is a **regression guard, not a bug reproduction** — which is precisely why
it must be mutation-checked: a green-on-arrival leg that cannot go RED is the failure class this chunk exists to
end (the discipline `2026-08-23-ingestion-scrub-coverage` established, per test-plan amendments).

Research made the assertion cheap and exact: the effect is **already observable and already allowlisted**.
`handle_close_to_tray` (`pulse-app/src/window.rs:119-129`) emits `ui.layout.transition` with `layout_mode_from`
(the sanitized window label), `layout_mode_to = "hidden"`, `tray_visible`, and the leaf at
`observability.rs:975` permits exactly those fields. **No new tracing target, no new allowlist leaf, no new
production code** — per obs-plan §8 and §4 Scenario P5, whose must-trace span this already is. The chunk claims
**no capability**: P-076 is the consumer and claims at its own chunk (the matrix link step is a deliberate no-op).

6. **Shut down cleanly on both arms** — by SPECIFIC pid (`powershell -NoProfile -Command "Stop-Process -Id <pid> -Force"`),
