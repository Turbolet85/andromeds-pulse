## Goal

Make the deterministic-L4 verification surface **gradeable**, and prove the incident read-back chain
end-to-end locally — the half of P-075 that is achievable at HEAD and independent of any external repo.
The canned L4 output hardcodes `"evidence_refs": []`, and that emptiness reaches two graded surfaces
through a single production join (`pulse-app/src/inference_runtime.rs:734` maps `L4Output.evidence_refs`
into `Incident.evidence_refs.fingerprint_hashes`): the MCP `retrieve_telemetry_slice.fingerprint_refs`
field and the Diagnostic Report's Evidence section. Any payload-identity or token assertion against those
surfaces compares nothing and passes, and — the sharper hazard — an ABSENCE check ("no evidence refs
leaked") passes for entirely the wrong reason and would keep passing after a real leak. Populating the
fixture converts both from vacuous-green to discriminating, in the exact mode chosen for e2e verification
precisely because it is reproducible.

Two research findings deliberately shape this chunk **smaller** than its route entry implies, and both are
recorded rather than papered over. First, `degraded_mode` is not a canned-output property at all: it is
`Report.degraded_mode`, computed as `parsed_l4.is_none()` from Resolved-only `resolution_summary_text`
(`incidents_router.rs:437-441`), so an Active incident renders degraded in real-model mode too — the CARRY
attributed it to the wrong layer, and no fixture change can move it. Second, of the four delegated timing
caps only **P-037** has an observable surface (`metric.report.render_ms`, `incidents_router.rs:561`);
P-025 / P-027 / P-045 have no `metric.*` target on the Pulse side and no latency assertion on the harness
side either. Third, the Conductor drive is blocked — on the scenario's **assertions**, not on storm
formation: `scenarios/fingerprint-storm.toml:9-13` encodes the superseded pre-token-leading normalization,
so at HEAD its variant triple SPLITS into two fingerprints (the line variant still shares the base, the
path variant stays relative and separates). Two storms form; what fails is the single-`fingerprint_hex`
premise the scenario now grades on, its per-phase tier boundaries, and its own
`same_fingerprint_variants_match_the_base` unit test — a repair executed in that repo, never from here.
See `research.md` §Q4 for the derivation and the correction notice on an earlier "no storm forms" draft.

Therefore this chunk **does not claim P-075**. It lands the Pulse-side gradeability fix, proves the chain
through the real producer and a real cross-process MCP read-back, and records the failed concretization on
the cap so the next phase does not re-derive the dead end (per `verification-matrix-contract.md`
§Acceptance lifecycle — "decline is deterministic"). Per operator decision at P4, the wider producer gap
(`trace_id` / `span_ids` / `timestamps_unix_nano` hardcoded empty in **every** mode) is recorded as a
finding for a future entry, not fixed here — keeping this a fixture-plus-proof change with no
production-behaviour delta.

## Implementation notes

- **The two `evidence_refs` are different types.** `L4Output.evidence_refs` is `Vec<String>`;
  `Incident.evidence_refs` is the `EvidenceRefs` struct. The vector becomes the struct's `fingerprint_hashes`
  field and nothing else (`inference_runtime.rs:734`). Do not expect the fixture to populate `span_ids`.
- **The MCP read-back and the IPC report project the same `Report`** (`markdown.rs::assemble_report`, moved
  there at chunk #94 for P-038 single-source), so one fixture fix serves both surfaces — but an **Active**
  incident renders through the `None` branch, where evidence appears as `` `fp:{…}` `` prefixed entries
  (`markdown.rs:316-321`), while a **Resolved** one renders through `Some(l4)` with the raw strings
  (`markdown.rs:286`). The read-back assertions must match the branch the seeded incident is actually in.
- **Order sensitivity — bindings.ts.** This chunk touches no TauRPC surface, but the gate chain includes
  default-features cargo runs that rewrite `pulse-app/ui/src/bindings/index.ts`. Per `rules/security.md`
  2026-08-15, re-run the mcp-server-feature regen AFTER the last cargo op and gate the commit on the STAGED
  copy (`git show :pulse-app/ui/src/bindings/index.ts | grep -c '"mcp":'` ≥ 1).
- **`cargo fmt` (not `--check`) once after the edit burst** — the PostToolUse rustfmt hook parses as edition
  2015 and disagrees with the edition-2024 gate (`rules/testing.md` 2026-08-14).
- **Expected amendments (wrap): none anticipated.** The chunk adds no env var, no IPC route, no event topic,
